# KPI Definitions
- **Applications**: unique, qualified submissions.
- **Acceptance rate**: accepted / qualified.
- **Founder NPS**: post‑program survey (0–10).
- **Follow‑on rate**: % teams that raise within 12 months.
- **Survival rate**: % active at 18 months.
- **Jobs created**: FTEs across cohort.
- **Capital raised**: aggregate post‑program.
