# Mentor Memorandum of Understanding (Template)

- **Role**: Provide domain expertise and feedback. No guarantee of outcomes.
- **Time**: 1–2 sessions per month during cohort.
- **Compensation**: Typically volunteer; optional recognition; no equity implied.
- **Confidentiality**: Respect founder confidentiality.
- **Conflict of Interest**: Disclose any conflicts; recuse if necessary.
- **Attribution**: Permission required for public listing as mentor.
- **Conduct**: Adhere to Code of Conduct and Anti‑Harassment Policy.
