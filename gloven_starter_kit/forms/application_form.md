# Founder Application — Questions (copy into your form tool)

**Basics**
- Startup name, one‑liner, website/demo link
- Founders (names, roles, emails, LinkedIn)
- Location(s) & time zones
- Contact phone / WhatsApp / Telegram

**Product & Problem**
- What problem do you solve and for whom? (max 200 words)
- Why now? (market shift, tech unlock, regulation, etc.)
- What is your insight or unfair advantage?

**Traction**
- Current stage (idea/prototype/MVP/revenue)
- Key metrics (MAUs, revenue, waitlist, pilots)
- Links to demo, repo, deck (optional)

**Business**
- Business model & pricing (if known)
- Competition / alternatives
- Moat hypothesis (3 sentences)

**Team**
- How long have you worked together?
- Relevant domain expertise
- Full‑time commitment plan & runway

**Program Fit**
- What do you want from Gloven in 12 weeks?
- Are you interested in optional $25k SAFE? (yes/no/maybe)

**Declarations**
- Company incorporated? Where?
- Prior funding? (amount, instrument)
- Legal/ethical constraints we should know
