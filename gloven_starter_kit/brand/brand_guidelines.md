# Gloven (Global Ventures) — Brand Guidelines

## Name & Story
**Gloven** blends **Glo**bal + **oven** (a warm place where raw ideas rise).  
**Global Ventures** is the master brand; **Gloven** is the friendly short name.

**Tagline options**
- *Where bold ideas rise to go global.*
- *Warmth for early sparks. Lift for global scale.*

## Mission
Accelerate mission‑driven founders worldwide with capital, coaching, and community that compound into enduring, category‑defining companies.

## Vision
A truly global founder network where geography and background do not limit potential.

## Values (the G‑L‑O‑V‑E‑N set)
- **Grit with empathy** — high standards, human approach.
- **Leverage the network** — compound effects beat heroics.
- **Open knowledge** — defaults to sharing.
- **Velocity with integrity** — speed without shortcuts.
- **Evidence‑based** — decisions from data + lived context.
- **No‑ego leadership** — we serve founders, not our vanity.

## Positioning
- **Who we serve:** pre‑seed / seed founders, globally distributed, software or tech‑enabled.
- **What we offer:** a 12‑week incubator + optional micro‑pre‑seed checks, world‑class mentors, and a global investor network.
- **Why we win:** founder‑first ops, a distributed mentor mesh, and rigorous playbooks.

## Programs
- **Gloven Incubator (12 weeks, non‑dilutive)** — hands‑on, curriculum + mentors.
- **Gloven Catalyst (optionally $25k SAFE for 2–3% MFN)** — for top teams.
- **Gloven Fellows (rolling)** — solo founders pre‑idea or pre‑team.

## Visual Identity
**Palette (accessible pairings)**
- Gloven Teal `#00A7A7` (primary)
- Warm Oven Coral `#FF6B6B` (accent)
- Charcoal `#1F2937` (text)
- Cloud `#F3F4F6` (background)
- Mint Edge `#A7F3D0` (secondary)

**Typography**
- Headlines: `Poppins` or `Inter` (700/600)
- Body: `Inter` or system UI (400/500)
- Monospace: `IBM Plex Mono`

**Logo usage (until a designer refines)**
- Simple wordmark “Gloven” in bold with a small rounded corner square “oven” glyph. Keep 16px padding. Never distort or recolor outside the palette.

## Voice
Warm, clear, evidence‑driven. Avoid jargon. Write for global English.
