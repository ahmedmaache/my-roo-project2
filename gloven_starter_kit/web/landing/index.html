FAQ

Do you take equity?

The incubator is non‑dilutive. Select teams may take optional $25k via SAFE with MFN; no obligation to accept.

What stages do you back?

Pre‑seed to seed, software or tech‑enabled. We welcome global, distributed teams.

Is the program remote or in person?

Remote‑first with optional local meetups and a hybrid Demo Day.
