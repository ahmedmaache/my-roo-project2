# 12‑Week Cohort Calendar (Template)

**Orientation (Week 0):** Tools, norms, Code of Conduct, baseline metrics.

- **W1 — Clarity:** ICP, jobs‑to‑be‑done, sharp problem statements.
- **W2 — Customer Discovery:** Interviews, survey discipline, early funnel.
- **W3 — Product:** MVP scope, riskiest assumptions, validation plan.
- **W4 — Design & UX:** Prototyping, usability tests, accessibility.
- **W5 — Tech & Data:** Architecture basics, roadmaps, analytics.
- **W6 — Go‑to‑Market I:** Narrative, positioning, pricing experiments.
- **W7 — Go‑to‑Market II:** Channels, partnerships, founder‑led sales.
- **W8 — Legal/Finance:** Entities, founder agreements, runway math.
- **W9 — Fundraising:** Story, traction proof, SAFE terms, targeting.
- **W10 — Execution Systems:** OKRs, weekly cadence, hiring V1.
- **W11 — Demo Day Prep:** Deck reviews, Q&A drills, data room.
- **W12 — Demo Day:** Pitches + investor 1:1s. Alumni retro & next‑milestones.
