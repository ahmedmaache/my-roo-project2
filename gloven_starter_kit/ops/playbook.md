# Operations Playbook — Gloven

## Core Systems (suggested)
- Email: Google Workspace (hello@gloven.org, apply@gloven.org, mentor@gloven.org)
- Knowledge base: Notion (handbook + program content)
- CRM: Airtable or HubSpot (applications, mentors, investors)
- Community: Slack or Discord (with onboarding bot + #demo‑day updates)
- Scheduling: Calendly / Cal.com + Zoom / Google Meet
- Analytics: Plausible or Google Analytics (landing); Mixpanel (product‑level, optional)
- Storage: Google Drive (legal, founder docs)

## Weekly Rhythm (during cohort)
- **Mon**: Goals & commitments (30m) — each team posts 1–3 measurable goals.
- **Wed**: Expert sessions / office hours.
- **Fri**: Ship review (45–60m) — show what shipped; score with green/amber/red.

## Decision cadence
- Applications batched weekly; rubric scored by 2 reviewers min.
- Investment decisions by an IC (3 people) using IC memo template.
- Post‑mortem after each cohort: what to cut, what to double down on.
