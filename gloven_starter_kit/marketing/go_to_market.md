# Go‑to‑Market Plan (Launch → Cohort 01)

## Audience
- Primary: pre‑seed software founders (0–12 months in), globally distributed.
- Secondary: operator‑mentors, pre‑seed investors, university labs.

## Channels
- Content (weekly essays + founder stories)
- Social (LinkedIn, X/Twitter) — founder tips, mentor quotes, cohort updates
- Community talks (universities, meetups, dev communities)
- Partner perks & co‑marketing
- PR: launch note + demo day coverage

## 6‑Week Launch Sprint
- W‑6: Tease brand + collect mentor interest
- W‑5: Publish “Why Gloven” + application date
- W‑4: Release landing page & form; start founder office hours
- W‑3: Announce first mentors/partners (3–5 names)
- W‑2: Share sample curriculum; testimonials from early advisors
- W‑1: Final reminder, AMA livestream

**KPI targets:** 300 qualified applications, 8–12 teams selected, mentor bench 40+.
