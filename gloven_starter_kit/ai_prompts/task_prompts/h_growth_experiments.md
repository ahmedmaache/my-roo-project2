## TASK

Generate 10 growth experiments to increase qualified applications to Gloven.

## CONSTRAINTS

- **Budget:** Non-dilutive or low-cost (≤$500/experiment)
- **Timeline:** Quick to validate (≤2 weeks each)
- **Resources:** Small team (2–3 people)
- **Geography:** Global time zones

## OUTPUT_FORMAT

Markdown table with columns:

| # | Hypothesis | Channel | Setup Steps | Success Metric & Target | Est. Cost | Effort | ICE Score |
|---|------------|---------|-------------|-------------------------|-----------|--------|-----------|

**Column details:**
- **#:** Experiment number (1–10)
- **Hypothesis:** "If we [action], then [expected outcome] because [reasoning]"
- **Channel:** Content, Social, Partnerships, Email, Community, Paid, Referral
- **Setup Steps:** 3–5 concrete actions to launch
- **Success Metric & Target:** Specific KPI with numeric goal (e.g., "50 qualified apps from X")
- **Est. Cost:** $0, $50–$200, $200–$500
- **Effort:** S (small, <4 hours), M (medium, 4–12 hours), L (large, 12+ hours)
- **ICE Score:** Impact (1–10) × Confidence (1–10) × Ease (1–10) / 100 = 0–10

### Follow-up Section

After the table, provide:

1. **Top 3 Recommendations** — Which experiments to run first and why
2. **Measurement Dashboard** — What to track weekly (in bullet format)
3. **Kill Criteria** — When to stop an underperforming experiment

## ICE SCORING GUIDE

- **Impact (1–10):** How much will this move the KPI if it works?
  - 1–3 = Marginal (10–50 apps)
  - 4–7 = Meaningful (50–150 apps)
  - 8–10 = Game-changing (150+ apps)

- **Confidence (1–10):** How sure are you this will work?
  - 1–3 = Wild guess, no precedent
  - 4–7 = Some evidence, tested elsewhere
  - 8–10 = High confidence, proven playbook

- **Ease (1–10):** How easy to execute?
  - 1–3 = Complex, requires new tools/skills
  - 4–7 = Moderate, uses existing resources
  - 8–10 = Simple, can launch this week

## QUALITY CHECK

Before finalizing, ensure:
- ✅ Each hypothesis is testable and specific
- ✅ Success metrics are numeric and time-bound
- ✅ Setup steps are actionable (not vague)
- ✅ ICE scores are calculated correctly
- ✅ Mix of channels (not all content or all paid)
- ✅ Experiments are independent (can run in parallel)
- ✅ Cost estimates are realistic
- ✅ Effort levels match team capacity
