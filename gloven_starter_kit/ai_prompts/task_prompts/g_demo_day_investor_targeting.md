## TASK

Build an investor target list and outreach plan for Gloven's cohort Demo Day.

## CONTEXT

- **Cohort focus:** [e.g., "SaaS, AI/ML, fintech"]
- **Stage:** Pre-seed to seed
- **Check sizes:** $100k–$1M
- **Regions:** [e.g., "US, EU, LATAM"]
- **Demo Day date:** [Date]
- **Number of teams:** [8–12]

## OUTPUT_FORMAT

### Part 1: Investor Target List (30–50 investors)

Markdown table with columns:

| Firm/Angel | Stage | Check Size | Sector Fit | Geography | Why Fit | Intro Path | Priority |
|------------|-------|------------|------------|-----------|---------|------------|----------|

**Priority levels:**
- `A` = Perfect fit, warm intro available
- `B` = Good fit, cold outreach
- `C` = Stretch fit, nurture for future

Include mix of:
- Micro VCs / pre-seed funds
- Angel syndicates
- Individual angels with operator backgrounds
- Regional funds
- Corporate VCs (if relevant)

### Part 2: Outreach Templates

**A) Intro Request to Mutual Contact (≤120 words)**
```
Subject: Quick intro request — Gloven Demo Day
Body with:
- Context on the mutual connection
- Brief on Gloven and cohort
- Specific investor fit
- Easy ask (double opt-in intro)
```

**B) Cold Outreach to Investor (≤150 words)**
```
Subject: [Startup-specific hook] — Gloven Demo Day invite
Body with:
- Personalized opening (their portfolio/thesis fit)
- 2–3 traction bullets from cohort
- Demo Day logistics
- Clear CTA (RSVP, calendar link)
```

### Part 3: Timeline & Cadence (T-28 to T+14 days)

Table format:

| Day | Action | Owner | Tool/Channel |
|-----|--------|-------|--------------|
| T-28 | Finalize target list | Program Director | Airtable |
| T-21 | Send intro requests | Team | Email |
| ... | ... | ... | ... |

### Part 4: Data Room Checklist

Bullet list of what each startup should prepare:
- [ ] Pitch deck (PDF, ≤12 slides)
- [ ] Demo video (≤2 min)
- [ ] One-pager (financial snapshot)
- [ ] Key metrics dashboard
- [ ] Cap table
- [ ] FAQ doc
- [ ] Team bios

## QUALITY CHECK

Before finalizing, ensure:
- ✅ Investor fit is specific (not "invests in tech")
- ✅ Check sizes align with startup needs
- ✅ Geographic distribution matches cohort
- ✅ Intro paths are realistic and trackable
- ✅ Email templates have personalization hooks
- ✅ Timeline has clear owners and deadlines
- ✅ Data room checklist is founder-actionable
