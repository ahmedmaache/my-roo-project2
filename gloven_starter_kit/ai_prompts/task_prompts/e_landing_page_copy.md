## TASK

Write above-the-fold copy for Gloven's homepage plus 3 proof elements and an FAQ.

## CONTEXT

- **Page goal:** Convert visitors → application starts
- **Audience:** Pre-seed founders (0–12 months in), globally distributed
- **Key differentiators:** Non-dilutive, global access, evidence-based, 12-week structure
- **Objections to address:** "Why not Y Combinator?", "Is this remote-friendly?", "Do you take equity?"

## OUTPUT_FORMAT

Markdown with these sections:

### 1. Hero Section
```
H1: [Main headline — benefit-driven, 8–12 words]
Subhead: [2 lines clarifying the offer and audience]
CTA Primary: [Button text]
CTA Secondary: [Link text]
```

### 2. Social Proof (3 bullets)
Format: `"Quote or stat" — Attribution`

Examples:
- Alumni outcomes
- Mentor credentials
- Program metrics

### 3. Program Snapshot
4–6 bullets explaining:
- What you get
- Time commitment
- Non-dilutive structure
- Optional capital
- Global access

### 4. FAQ (5 Q&As)
Cover:
- Equity/dilution
- Stage fit
- Remote vs in-person
- Application timeline
- Acceptance criteria

### 5. Footer Tagline
One punchy line that reinforces the mission.

### 6. Accessibility Pass
Brief note on readability level (target: CEFR B2, global English).

## QUALITY CHECK

Before finalizing, ensure:
- ✅ H1 is clear, specific, benefit-driven (not clever/vague)
- ✅ Subhead explains WHO and WHAT in 2 lines
- ✅ CTAs are action-oriented ("Apply now" > "Learn more")
- ✅ Proof elements are specific and credible
- ✅ FAQ addresses real objections
- ✅ No jargon, idioms, or culturally-specific references
- ✅ Global English (explain acronyms, avoid regional slang)
- ✅ Tone is warm + professional (not hype-y)
