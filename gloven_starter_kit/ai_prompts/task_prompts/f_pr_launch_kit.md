## TASK

Create a press kit for Gloven's Cohort 01 launch.

## CONTEXT

- **Launch angle:** [e.g., "Global incubator for underrepresented founders", "AI-focused accelerator", "Remote-first program"]
- **News hook:** [e.g., "First cohort announcement", "Partnership with X", "Funding milestone"]
- **Key stats:** [e.g., "300 applications from 40 countries", "40 mentors from top companies"]
- **Spokesperson:** [Name, Title]
- **Embargo date:** [Date or "For Immediate Release"]

## OUTPUT_FORMAT

### Part 1: Press Release (400–600 words)

Structure:
```
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE — [Date]

[HEADLINE — News-worthy, specific]

[CITY] — [Opening paragraph with who/what/when/where/why]

[2–3 body paragraphs with details, context, quotes]

[Quote from spokesperson]

[Optional: Quote from partner/mentor/founder]

[Boilerplate about Gloven]

Contact:
[Name]
[Email]
[Phone]
```

### Part 2: Media List (15–20 outlets)

Markdown table with columns:

| Outlet | Reporter | Beat | Why Fit | Angle | Contact Hint | Priority |
|--------|----------|------|---------|-------|--------------|----------|

Include mix of:
- Tech/startup outlets (TechCrunch, VentureBeat, etc.)
- Regional/global business press
- Vertical publications (AI, fintech, climate, etc.)
- Podcasts/newsletters

### Part 3: Pitch Emails

**A) Initial Pitch (≤140 words)**
```
Subject: [News hook in 6–8 words]
Body with:
- Opening hook (why now, why relevant to their beat)
- 1–2 key facts/stats
- Offer (exclusive, interview, data access)
- One-sentence boilerplate
- Clear ask and timeline
```

**B) Follow-up (≤80 words)**
```
Subject: Re: [Original subject]
Body:
- Gentle reminder
- New angle or stat
- Reinforced offer
- Timeline
```

### Part 4: FAQ for Press (5 Q&As)

Cover:
- What is Gloven?
- How is this different from YC/Techstars?
- Who can apply?
- What's the acceptance rate?
- How do you make money?

## QUALITY CHECK

Before finalizing, ensure:
- ✅ Headline is newsworthy (not "Company X announces Y")
- ✅ Opening paragraph has all 5 Ws
- ✅ Quotes are human and specific (not generic)
- ✅ Boilerplate is 2–3 sentences max
- ✅ Media list has variety (not just tech press)
- ✅ Angles are personalized to each outlet/beat
- ✅ Pitch emails are concise and value-focused
- ✅ No hype language ("revolutionary", "game-changing")
- ✅ All claims are backed by facts/stats
