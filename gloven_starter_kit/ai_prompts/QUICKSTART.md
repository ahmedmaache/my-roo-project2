# 🚀 Gloven AI Growth Prompts — 5-Minute Quickstart

Get your first AI-generated growth output in under 5 minutes.

---

## Step 1: Choose Your LLM (30 seconds)

Open one of these:
- **ChatGPT** → https://chat.openai.com (GPT-4 recommended)
- **Claude** → https://claude.ai (Claude 3.5 Sonnet recommended)
- **Gemini** → https://gemini.google.com (Gemini 1.5 Pro recommended)

---

## Step 2: Set System Prompt (60 seconds)

1. Click **Settings** or **Custom Instructions** (varies by platform)
2. Find the **System** or **Developer** message field
3. Copy the entire contents of [`growth_os_system_prompt.md`](growth_os_system_prompt.md)
4. Paste into the System field
5. Save

**Note:** Some platforms call this "Custom Instructions" or "System Message"

---

## Step 3: Run Your First Task (90 seconds)

### Option A: Score a Startup Application

1. Open [`task_prompts/d_application_scoring.md`](task_prompts/d_application_scoring.md)
2. Copy the prompt
3. Replace the `[Insert application...]` section with this example:

```
Startup name: EcoTrack
One-liner: Carbon tracking for small businesses
Founders: Maria Lopez (CEO, ex-climate policy analyst), James Kim (CTO, full-stack dev)
Location: Mexico City
Problem: SMBs need simple carbon accounting for compliance and ESG reporting
Solution: Mobile app that tracks energy, travel, procurement → generates reports
Traction: 200 waitlist signups, 5 pilot customers, pre-revenue
Business model: $49-$199/month SaaS
Why Gloven: Need help with B2B sales and US market entry
```

4. Paste into your LLM
5. Hit Enter

**Expected output:** JSON score with GO/NO_GO recommendation in ~30 seconds

---

### Option B: Generate Social Media Content

1. Use this micro-prompt:

```
/thread
```

2. Wait ~30 seconds

**Expected output:** 6-tweet thread about Gloven being non-dilutive + founder story + CTA

---

## Step 4: Iterate (60 seconds)

Try refining:

```
Make tweet #3 more technical and add a stat about acceptance rates
```

Or:

```
Turn this thread into a LinkedIn post (150 words)
```

## Common Issues

**Q: The output is too generic**  
A: Add more context to your task prompt (regions, dates, specific goals)

**Q: It's not following the format**  
A: Double-check you pasted the full system prompt, not just part of it

**Q: The tone isn't right**  
A: Edit the `AUDIENCE & VOICE` section in the system prompt

**Q: I need it in a different language**  
A: Add to your task: "Output in [language], but keep technical terms in English"
